# Wortschatz B2: Vocabulary

Target: **~4,000 words** (estimate — there is no official Goethe list at this level). From B2 up, vocabulary becomes **topic-driven**: learn the words of your profession and your exam topics.

This file covers the general academic, formal, and abstract vocabulary that B2 texts assume. Combined with A1-A2-B1 (~800 listed so far), it moves you toward the B2 range.

## How to use this file

- Learn nouns **with article and plural**.
- Learn verbs **with preposition and case**.
- Learn the **Funktionsverbgefüge** as fixed phrases.
- Add your own field's vocabulary (IT, medicine, law, engineering...) separately.
