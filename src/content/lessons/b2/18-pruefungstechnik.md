# Lektion 18: Prüfungstechnik — Alle vier Module

**Level:** B2 | **Target time:** 30 minutes, repeat over 2 sessions | **Status:** not started

---

## Goal

Know the structure of the Goethe B2 and TestDaF exams, and use proven techniques for each module.

## Why this matters

B2 is your goal for work or study. Knowing the exam format is not cheating; it is preparation. Each module has a predictable structure and time budget, and the right technique saves points you would otherwise lose to panic or poor time management.

## Material

### The exams at this level

| Exam | Use | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Goethe-Zertifikat B2 | general, work | modular, all four skills |
| telc Deutsch B2 | general, work | similar structure |
| TestDaF | university | graded TDN 3-5; TDN 4 ≈ B2/C1 |
| DSH | university | university entrance |

**For university**, TestDaF or DSH is usually required, not Goethe B2.

### Goethe B2: the four modules

| Module | Time | Parts |
|---|---|---|
| Lesen | 65 min | 5 parts |
| Hören | 40 min | 4 parts |
| Schreiben | 75 min | 2 tasks |
| Sprechen | ~15 min | 2 parts (presentation + discussion) |

You can take modules separately.

### Lesen (reading)

**What it tests:** understanding of longer articles, opinion pieces, and formal texts.

**Techniques:**
1. **Skim first** — read the title and first lines to get the topic.
2. **Read the questions** before the text, so you know what to look for.
3. **Do not translate** — find the answer, move on.
4. **Watch for synonyms** — the text rarely uses the exact question word.
5. **Time budget** — about 13 minutes per part.
6. **Never leave blank** — guess if unsure.

**Common trap:** an answer that is *almost* right. Check whether the text really says it.

### Hören (listening)

**What it tests:** announcements, conversations, interviews, talks.

**Techniques:**
1. **Read the questions during the pause** before each part.
2. **Listen for keywords** in the question.
3. **Do not panic** if you miss a word; keep up with the flow.
4. **Note down numbers, names, times** as you hear them.
5. **The second listen** usually fills the gaps.
6. **Never leave blank.**

**Practice material:** Tagesschau, Deutschlandfunk, Easy German, slowly spoken news (Nachrichtenleicht).

### Schreiben (writing)

**What it tests:** a formal letter/email and an argumentative text or forum post.

**Task 1 (formal):** a letter/email (complaint, request, application).
**Task 2 (opinion):** an argumentative post or essay.

**Techniques:**
1. **Plan 5 minutes** before writing.
2. **Use the structure** from Lektion 16.
3. **Answer every content point** in the task — missing one loses marks.
4. **Use connectors** (deshalb, außerdem, trotzdem).
5. **Keep the register consistent.**
6. **Reserve 5 minutes** to check cases, verb positions, and register.

**Marking criteria:** task fulfilment, coherence, vocabulary, grammar.

### Sprechen (speaking)

**What it tests:** a presentation and a discussion.

**Techniques:**
1. **Use a fixed structure** for the presentation (Lektion 17).
2. **Use signposting phrases** so the examiner can follow.
3. **In the discussion**, engage with your partner, not just the examiner.
4. **Soften disagreement** with the Konjunktiv II.
5. **Keep going** — fluency matters more than perfection.
6. **Do not memorise a script**; memorised speeches sound unnatural and can derail.

**Marking criteria:** fluency, coherence, vocabulary, grammar, interaction.

### General exam strategy

| Principle | Detail |
|---|---|
| Know the format | Practise with real past papers |
| Time budget | Allocate minutes per part; stick to them |
| Never blank | A guess is better than nothing |
| Read the task twice | Half the errors are task misreads |
| Register | Match the text type |
| Check at the end | Reserve 5 minutes |

### TestDaF specifics (if university is the goal)

| Part | Note |
|---|---|
| Leseverstehen | 3 tasks |
| Hörverstehen | 3 tasks |
| Schriftlicher Ausdruck | 1 task (graph + argument) |
| Mündlicher Ausdruck | 7 tasks, recorded |
| Grading | TDN 3, 4, or 5; TDN 4 in all four = B2/C1 |

**TestDaF is academic**, so learn academic vocabulary for your field.

### Practical B2 rules

1. **Practise with real past papers**, timed.
2. **Plan writing tasks**; do not start immediately.
3. **Never leave a blank** in reading or listening.
4. **Match the register** to the text type.
5. **For university**, plan for TestDaF/DSH, not Goethe B2.

## Exercises

**1. Which module?**

a) You read a longer article and answer questions. → ______
b) You listen to an interview and take notes. → ______
c) You write a formal letter and an opinion text. → ______
d) You give a presentation and discuss. → ______

**2. Technique check.**

a) In Lesen, should you translate the whole text? → ______
b) In Hören, should you leave a blank if unsure? → ______
c) In Schreiben, how long should you plan? → ______
d) In Sprechen, should you memorise a script? → ______

**3. Which exam for university?**

a) Goethe B2 → ______
b) TestDaF → ______
c) telc B2 → ______

**4. Match the marking criterion to the module.**

a) Task fulfilment → ______
b) Fluency → ______
c) Coherence → ______ (both)

**5. Time budget.**

a) Lesen (Goethe B2): ______ min
b) Hören: ______ min
c) Schreiben: ______ min

**6. Translate into German.**

a) I have to practise with real past papers.
b) Never leave a blank.
c) I plan the writing task for five minutes.
d) For university I need TestDaF.
e) The presentation has a fixed structure.

**7. Fix the mistake (exam strategy).**

a) Starting to write immediately without planning.
b) Translating the whole reading text word by word.
c) Memorising a speech for the presentation.
d) Leaving answers blank when unsure.

## Pronunciation note

Exam vocabulary: `Prüfung` (PRÜ-fung), `Modul` (mo-DUL), `Kriterium` (kri-TE-ri-um), `Flüssigkeit` (FLÜS-sig-kait).

## Common mistakes

- Wrong: not practising with real past papers.
- Wrong: poor time management; spending too long on one part.
- Wrong: leaving blanks.
- Wrong: mismatching register in the writing task.
- Wrong: memorising a presentation script.
