# Lektion 07: Extended Participial Attributes (Erweiterte Partizipialattribute)

**Level:** B2 | **Target time:** 30 minutes, repeat over 3 sessions | **Status:** not started

---

## Goal

Read and produce the long noun phrases that dominate formal German, where information is packed before the noun.

## Why this matters

This is the structure that makes German news and academic text hard to read. A noun phrase can carry an entire clause of information before the noun. Once you can parse these, your reading speed doubles; once you can write them, your formal writing sounds professional. It is a classic B2/C1 reading obstacle.

---

## Material

### The structure

A long attribute sits **between the article and the noun**, ending with a participle that carries the adjective ending.

`die **von der Regierung beschlossenen** Maßnahmen`

Breakdown:
- `die` — article
- `von der Regierung` — extra information (agent)
- `beschlossenen` — participle + adjective ending
- `Maßnahmen` — the noun

= "the measures decided by the government"

### Why German does this

German prefers to pack information **before** the noun. English uses a relative clause after it.

| German (attribute) | English (relative clause) |
|---|---|
| die von der Regierung beschlossenen Maßnahmen | the measures that were decided by the government |
| der in Berlin lebende Künstler | the artist who lives in Berlin |
| das gestern gekaufte Auto | the car that was bought yesterday |
| die seit Jahren steigenden Preise | the prices that have been rising for years |

### How to read them: the backwards method

Start at the **noun**, then work leftwards.

`die **von der Regierung beschlossenen** Maßnahmen`
1. Noun: Maßnahmen (measures)
2. Participle: beschlossenen (decided)
3. Extra info: von der Regierung (by the government)

= the measures decided by the government

### Types of extra information

| Type | Example | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Agent (von) | die von Goethe geschriebenen Werke | works written by Goethe |
| Place | der in Berlin lebende Künstler | the artist living in Berlin |
| Time | das gestern gekaufte Auto | the car bought yesterday |
| Duration | die seit Jahren steigenden Preise | prices rising for years |
| Object | die das Problem lösende Methode | the method solving the problem |
| Prepositional | die auf dem Tisch liegenden Bücher | the books lying on the table |
| Negation | die nicht zu unterschätzende Gefahr | the danger not to be underestimated |

### More examples

| German | English |
|---|---|
| der seit 20 Jahren bestehende Verein | the club existing for 20 years |
| die von vielen Menschen genutzte App | the app used by many people |
| das in der Zeitung veröffentlichte Interview | the interview published in the newspaper |
| die an der Universität arbeitenden Forscher | the researchers working at the university |
| der durch den Unfall entstandene Schaden | the damage caused by the accident |
| die mit großer Mehrheit getroffene Entscheidung | the decision taken by a large majority |
| der noch zu klärende Punkt | the point still to be clarified |

### The same structure with zu + Partizip I

| German | English |
|---|---|
| die zu lösende Aufgabe | the task to be solved |
| die bis morgen zu erledigende Arbeit | the work to be completed by tomorrow |
| die nicht zu vermeidenden Kosten | the unavoidable costs |

### Producing them

To produce an extended attribute:

1. Start from a relative clause.
2. Turn the verb into a participle.
3. Move the extra information before the participle.
4. Add the adjective ending.

| Step | Result |
|---|---|
| Relative clause | die Maßnahmen, die von der Regierung beschlossen wurden |
| Participle | die ... beschlossenen Maßnahmen |
| Extra info moved | die von der Regierung beschlossenen Maßnahmen |

### More transformations

| Relative clause | Extended attribute |
|---|---|
| der Künstler, der in Berlin lebt | der in Berlin lebende Künstler |
| das Auto, das gestern gekauft wurde | das gestern gekaufte Auto |
| die Preise, die seit Jahren steigen | die seit Jahren steigenden Preise |
| die App, die von vielen genutzt wird | die von vielen genutzte App |
| das Problem, das gelöst werden muss | das zu lösende Problem |

### When to use extended attributes

| Use them in... | Avoid them in... |
|---|---|
| formal reports | casual speech |
| academic writing | personal emails |
| news articles | instructions |
| legal text | storytelling |
| exam writing (for style) | anything meant to be clear and simple |

**Warning:** too many extended attributes make text unreadable. German itself warns against "Substantivitis" (over-nominalisation). Use them for effect, not everywhere.

### Reading strategy for long phrases

1. Find the article (`der/die/das`).
2. Find the noun at the end of the phrase.
3. Identify the participle just before the noun.
4. Read the extra information between.
5. Reconstruct as a relative clause in your head.

`die **mit großer Mehrheit getroffene** Entscheidung`
→ decision ← taken ← with a large majority
= the decision taken by a large majority

## Pronunciation note

Read the phrase as one unit, with the main stress on the noun: `die von der Regierung beschlossenen MASSnahmen`. Keep the intervening words light and quick.

## Common mistakes

- Wrong: putting the extra info after the noun in a formal text. Right: before the participle.
- Wrong: forgetting the adjective ending on the participle.
- Wrong: `die zu lösen Aufgabe`. Right: `die zu lösende Aufgabe`.
- Wrong: overusing extended attributes in speech; they belong to writing.
- Wrong: losing the noun in a very long phrase. Keep phrases readable.
