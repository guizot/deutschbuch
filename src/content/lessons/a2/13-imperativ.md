# Lektion 13: Der Imperativ (The Imperative)

**Level:** A2 | **Target time:** 30 minutes, repeat over 3 sessions | **Status:** not started

---

## Goal

Give commands, instructions, requests, and advice in the correct form for each person.

## Why this matters

The imperative is everyday German: instructions, recipes, directions, requests, warnings. It also appears in exams (writing instructions, giving advice) and in reading (signs, manuals). It is short and useful.

---

## Material

### Four forms of the imperative

German has imperatives for **du**, **ihr**, **Sie**, and **wir**.

| Person | Form | Example (machen) |
|---|---|---|
| du | stem (often no ending) | Mach! |
| ihr | stem + t | Macht! |
| Sie | infinitive + Sie | Machen Sie! |
| wir | infinitive + wir | Machen wir! |

### The du-form

Take the du-present form, remove the -st ending, and drop the pronoun.

| Infinitive | du-present | Imperative (du) |
|---|---|---|
| machen | du machst | **Mach!** |
| lernen | du lernst | **Lern!** |
| kommen | du kommst | **Komm!** |
| gehen | du gehst | **Geh!** |
| spielen | du spielst | **Spiel!** |

**For verbs with a vowel change** (a → ä, e → i/ie), the change **stays** in the imperative.

| Infinitive | du-present | Imperative (du) |
|---|---|---|
| fahren | du fährst | **Fahr!** (a→ä does NOT stay) |
| schlafen | du schläfst | **Schlaf!** (a→ä does NOT stay) |
| essen | du isst | **Iss!** (e→i stays) |
| sprechen | du sprichst | **Sprich!** (e→i stays) |
| lesen | du liest | **Lies!** (e→ie stays) |
| nehmen | du nimmst | **Nimm!** (e→i stays) |
| geben | du gibst | **Gib!** (e→i stays) |
| sehen | du siehst | **Sieh!** (e→ie stays) |

**Rule:** the e → i / ie change stays; the a → ä change drops back to a.

**Extra -e (optional, formal):** Some verbs can add -e: `Mache!`, `Gehe!`, `Komme!`. This sounds slightly more formal or literary. In speech, the short form (`Mach!`, `Geh!`) is normal.

**Verbs ending in -eln/-ern:** add -e. `sammeln` → `Sammle!`, `wandern` → `Wandere!`

**Verbs ending in -ten/-den:** keep the extra e. `arbeiten` → `Arbeite!`, `reden` → `Rede!`

### The ihr-form

Use the ihr-present form without the pronoun.

| Infinitive | ihr-present | Imperative (ihr) |
|---|---|---|
| machen | ihr macht | **Macht!** |
| lernen | ihr lernt | **Lernt!** |
| kommen | ihr kommt | **Kommt!** |
| essen | ihr esst | **Esst!** |
| fahren | ihr fahrt | **Fahrt!** |

No vowel change in the ihr form. `Esst!` not `Isst!`

### The Sie-form (formal)

Infinitive + Sie. Word order flips: verb first.

| Statement | Imperative (Sie) |
|---|---|
| Sie machen das. | **Machen Sie** das! |
| Sie kommen hier. | **Kommen Sie** hier! |
| Sie warten. | **Warten Sie**! |

The pronoun `Sie` stays. This is the polite form for strangers, officials, customers.

### The wir-form ("let's")

Infinitive + wir.

`**Gehen wir**!` (Let's go!)
`**Machen wir** das!` (Let's do that!)
`**Warten wir**!` (Let's wait!)

### Separable verbs

The prefix goes to the end.

| Infinitive | Imperative (du) |
|---|---|
| aufstehen | **Steh auf!** |
| anrufen | **Ruf an!** |
| mitkommen | **Komm mit!** |
| aufmachen | **Mach auf!** |
| zumachen | **Mach zu!** |
| einsteigen | **Steig ein!** |
| aussteigen | **Steig aus!** |
| aufpassen | **Pass auf!** |

### sein and haben (irregular imperative)

| | sein | haben |
|---|---|---|
| du | **Sei!** | **Hab!** (or Habe!) |
| ihr | **Seid!** | **Habt!** |
| Sie | **Seien Sie!** | **Haben Sie!** |
| wir | **Seien wir!** | **Haben wir!** |

`**Sei** ruhig!` (Be quiet!)
`**Seid** vorsichtig!` (Be careful! — plural)
`**Seien Sie** so nett.` (Be so kind. — formal)

### Politeness: softening commands

Bare imperatives can sound harsh. German softens them with particles.

| German | Nuance |
|---|---|
| Komm! | Come! (neutral, direct) |
| Komm mal! | Come (a moment), casual |
| Komm doch! | Come on, do come (encouraging) |
| Komm bitte! | Please come |
| Könntest du kommen? | Could you come? (very polite) |
| Würden Sie bitte...? | Would you please...? |

**`bitte`** (please) is the basic softener. **`mal`** makes it casual. **`doch`** makes it encouraging.

### Instructions and signs

The imperative is common in public signs, often in the infinitive instead.

| Sign | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Bitte nicht rauchen! | Please do not smoke! |
| Drücken! | Push! |
| Ziehen! | Pull! |
| Bitte anschnallen! | Please fasten your seatbelt! |
| Vorsicht! | Caution! |
| Betreten verboten! | No entry! |

### Commands with nouns and pronouns

Object pronouns come after the verb.

`Hilf **mir**!` (Help me!)
`Ruf **mich** an!` (Call me!)
`Geben Sie **mir** das Buch!` (Give me the book!)
`Setz **dich**!` (Sit down!)

## Pronunciation note

The du imperative is short and clipped: `Mach!`, `Komm!`. In `Sprich!` the ch is soft. In `Iss!` the s is sharp.

## Common mistakes

- Wrong: `Sprech!`. Right: `Sprich!`. The e→i change stays in the imperative.
- Wrong: `Fähr!`. Right: `Fahr!`. The a→ä change drops in the imperative.
- Wrong: including the pronoun: `Du komm!`. Right: `Komm!`.
- Wrong: `Aufsteh!`. Right: `Steh auf!`. Separable prefix goes to the end.
- Wrong: forgetting the irregular: `Sei` (not `Bin` or `Bist`).
