# Lektion 15: Der Genitiv (The Genitive Case)

**Level:** A2 | **Target time:** 30 minutes, repeat over 3 sessions | **Status:** not started

---

## Goal

Recognise and use the genitive for possession and after genitive prepositions.

## Why this matters

The genitive is the fourth case. At A2, you mainly need to **recognise** it and use it for simple possession, plus learn the genitive prepositions (`wegen`, `trotz`, `während`, `statt`). Full genitive mastery is a B1 skill, but the basics belong here because the prepositions are frequent.

---

## Material

### The genitive expresses possession

English uses "of" or " 's ". German uses the genitive.

`das Auto **des Mannes**` (the man's car / the car of the man)
`die Farbe **der Wand**` (the colour of the wall)
`der Titel **des Buches**` (the title of the book)

### Genitive articles

| | Masculine | Feminine | Neuter | Plural |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Definite | des | der | des | der |
| Indefinite | eines | einer | eines | - |
| Possessive (mein) | meines | meiner | meines | meiner |

### The masculine/neuter -s ending

Masculine and neuter nouns add **-s** or **-es** in the genitive.

| Nominative | Genitive |
|---|---|
| der Mann | des Mann**es** |
| das Kind | des Kind**es** |
| der Lehrer | des Lehrer**s** |
| das Auto | des Auto**s** |
| der Freund | des Freund**es** |

**-es** for one-syllable nouns (der Mann → des Mannes), **-s** for longer nouns (der Lehrer → des Lehrers). A few nouns always take -es: those ending in -s, -ss, -ß, -z, -tz, -sch.

Feminine and plural nouns have **no** ending change: `der Frau`, `der Frauen`.

### Genitive with names

For names, add **-s** to the name, and no article.

`**Rinaldis** Auto` (Rinaldi's car)
`**Annas** Buch` (Anna's book)
`**Toms** Schwester` (Tom's sister)

If the name ends in -s, use an apostrophe only: `**Hans'** Auto`.

### Genitive vs von

In spoken German, `von` + dative is often used instead of the genitive.

| Genitive (formal, written) | von + Dativ (spoken) |
|---|---|
| das Auto des Mannes | das Auto von dem Mann |
| die Farbe der Wand | die Farbe von der Wand |

**Both are correct.** The genitive sounds more formal and is standard in writing. The `von` form is common in speech. At A2, understand both, and use the genitive in writing.

### Genitive prepositions

These prepositions take the genitive.

| Preposition | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| wegen | because of | wegen **des** Wetters |
| trotz | despite | trotz **der** Kälte |
| während | during | während **des** Tages |
| statt / anstatt | instead of | statt **des** Autos |
| außerhalb | outside of | außerhalb **der** Stadt |
| innerhalb | inside of, within | innerhalb **einer** Woche |
| aufgrund | due to | aufgrund **des** Regens |

**Note:** in spoken German, these prepositions are sometimes used with the dative instead of the genitive. `wegen dem Wetter` is heard often but is considered incorrect by strict grammarians. Learn the genitive form.

### Genitive in everyday expressions

Some common phrases use the genitive and are worth memorising as fixed expressions:

| German | English |
|---|---|
| meiner Meinung nach | in my opinion |
| eines Tages | one day |
| letzten Endes | in the end |
| dieser Tage | these days |
| aller Art | of all kinds |

### When you will meet the genitive

| Context | Example |
|---|---|
| Possession in writing | die Hauptstadt Deutschlands |
| Formal titles | der Präsident der Universität |
| Newspaper headlines | wegen des Unfalls |
| Literature | die Stimme des Windes |
| Fixed expressions | meiner Meinung nach |

### Genitive pronouns (recognition)

Possessive pronouns in the genitive are rare. You may meet:
- `meinetwegen` (for my sake)
- `deinetwegen` (for your sake)
- `seinetwegen`, `ihretwegen`, `unseretwegen`

These are fixed words, not productive grammar. Recognise them.

### Practical advice for A2

1. **Recognise** the genitive in reading: `des Mannes`, `der Frau`.
2. **Use** the four main genitive prepositions: wegen, trotz, während, statt.
3. **Prefer** `von` + dative in casual speech.
4. Do not stress about full genitive production; that comes at B1.

## Pronunciation note

`des` is "des" (short). `wegen` is "VAY-gen". `trotz` is "trots". `während` is "VAY-rent".

## Common mistakes

- Wrong: `wegen das Wetter`. Right: `wegen des Wetters`. wegen takes the genitive.
- Wrong: adding an apostrophe-s to names: `Anna's Buch`. Right: `Annas Buch`.
- Wrong: forgetting the -s/-es on masculine/neuter nouns: `des Mann`. Right: `des Mannes`.
- Wrong: using the genitive where `von` is more natural in speech. Right: both are correct, but `von` is more casual.
- Wrong: adding endings to feminine/plural nouns: `des Frau`. Right: `der Frau`.
