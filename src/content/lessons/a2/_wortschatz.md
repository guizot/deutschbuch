# Wortschatz A2: Vocabulary

Target: **~1,300 words** (Goethe-Zertifikat A2 Wortliste). This is the cumulative total, so it includes the ~650 from A1.

This file lists the **new** vocabulary that typically appears at A2, grouped by theme. Add words you meet in Nicos Weg and Easy German to reach the full list.

## How to use this file

- Learn nouns **with article and plural**.
- Learn verbs **with their case pattern** (+Akk, +Dat) where relevant.
- Tick the box when the word is in your Anki deck.
