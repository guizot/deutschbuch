# Wortschatz C1: Vocabulary

Target: **~5,000-8,000 words** (estimate — no official list at this level). C1 vocabulary is **fully topic-driven**: general academic vocabulary plus the specialised vocabulary of your field and the topics you meet in exams and at work.

This file lists **general academic and formal C1 vocabulary** grouped by function. Add your own field-specific words as you meet them.

**Method:** at C1 you learn words **in context and in collocations**, not in lists. Every entry below should go into Anki as a phrase, not an isolated word.

## How to use this list

1. **Do not memorise it linearly.** Pick one functional group per week.
2. **Learn every word as a phrase** (verb + object, adjective + noun).
3. **Read widely** — newspapers (Die Zeit, Süddeutsche), academic articles, literature.
4. **Note collocations** in a separate column as you read.
5. **Add your own field vocabulary** in section 15; that is what matters most for your goal.
6. **Review with Anki** daily, 15-20 new cards.

## Recommended reading at C1

| Source | Level |
|---|---|
| Die Zeit | demanding, essayistic |
| Süddeutsche Zeitung | quality journalism |
| Deutschlandfunk | radio, interviews |
| Der Spiegel | long-form journalism |
| Academic journals in your field | specialist |
| Literature (e.g. Bernhard Schlink, Daniel Kehlmann) | literary German |

**Target:** by the C1 exam, you should recognise roughly 5,000-8,000 words, with deep knowledge of the ~3,000 most frequent.
