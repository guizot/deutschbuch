# Lektion 07: Kollokationen

**Level:** C1 | **Target time:** 30 minutes, repeat over 3 sessions | **Status:** not started

---

## Goal

Use the words that naturally go together, and stop producing grammatically correct but unnatural combinations.

## Why this matters

Collocation is what makes German sound native. You can be grammatically perfect and still sound foreign by choosing the "wrong" partner word: `eine Entscheidung machen` instead of `treffen`, `starke Kritik` instead of `scharfe Kritik`. At C1, collocation is the main remaining difference between good and excellent German.

---

## Material

### What a collocation is

A pair or group of words that habitually occur together, without being fully predictable.

`eine Entscheidung **treffen**` (not `machen`)
`eine Rolle **spielen**` (not `haben`)
`Kritik **üben**` (not `machen`)
`in Frage **stellen**` (not `setzen`)

The meaning is predictable, but the partner word is fixed by convention.

### Collocation vs idiom

| Type | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Collocation | predictable meaning, fixed partner | eine Entscheidung treffen |
| Idiom | unpredictable meaning | ins Fettnäpfchen treten |

Both are fixed; the idiom's meaning is opaque, the collocation's is not.

### Learning strategy

1. **Learn words in pairs**, not alone: "Entscheidung treffen", not "Entscheidung".
2. **Note the collocations** when reading; they are the most valuable notes.
3. **Use a collocation dictionary** (e.g. DWDS, Ozdic) when unsure.
4. **Group by noun**: what verbs go with `Kritik`? (üben, äußern, zurückweisen)
5. **Fix your own errors**: if a tutor corrects a collocation, add it to Anki.

### Practical C1 rules

1. **Learn verb + noun pairs** as units.
2. **Watch adjective choice**: German uses different adjectives than English.
3. **Beware of English influence** on verb choice.
4. **Note collocations** in every text you read.
5. **Prefer the fixed partner** even when another seems logical.

## Pronunciation note

In collocations, the noun usually carries the main stress: `eine EntSCHEIdung treffen`, `eine ROlle spielen`, `scharfe KRItik`. Say the pair as one unit.

## Common mistakes

- Wrong: `eine Entscheidung machen`. Right: `treffen`.
- Wrong: `eine Rolle haben`. Right: `spielen`.
- Wrong: English-influenced adjectives (`starke Kosten`). Right: `hohe Kosten`.
- Wrong: `Kritik machen`. Right: `Kritik üben`.
- Wrong: learning words in isolation instead of in pairs.
