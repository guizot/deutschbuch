# Lektion 18: Pronominaladverbien (da- und wo- Compounds)

**Level:** B1 | **Target time:** 30 minutes, repeat over 3 sessions | **Status:** not started

---

## Goal

Use da- and wo- compounds to refer to things, and know when to use them instead of preposition + pronoun.

## Why this matters

German avoids saying "for it", "about it", "with it" literally. Instead it builds compounds: `dafür`, `darüber`, `damit`. The same forms with `wo-` ask the question. This is essential for fluent speech and a frequent exam topic.

---

## Material

### The concept

When a preposition refers to a **thing** (not a person), German uses a `da-` compound.

| English | Wrong literal | Correct German |
|---|---|---|
| I am waiting for it. | Ich warte für es. | Ich warte **darauf**. |
| I think about it. | Ich denke über es. | Ich denke **daran**. |
| I am interested in it. | Ich interessiere mich für es. | Ich interessiere mich **dafür**. |

### Building da- compounds

`da` + preposition. If the preposition starts with a vowel, add **r**.

| Preposition | da- form |
|---|---|
| auf | darauf |
| an | daran |
| über | darüber |
| um | darum |
| von | davon |
| mit | damit |
| bei | dabei |
| für | dafür |
| gegen | dagegen |
| durch | dadurch |
| in | darin |
| zu | dazu |
| nach | danach |
| vor | davor |

**Rule:** `da` + consonant-initial preposition → just join (`damit`, `dafür`). `da` + vowel-initial preposition → add r (`darauf`, `daran`, `darüber`).

### Building wo- compounds (questions)

Same rule with `wo`.

| Preposition | wo- form |
|---|---|
| auf | worauf |
| an | woran |
| über | worüber |
| um | worum |
| von | wovon |
| mit | womit |
| bei | wobei |
| für | wofür |
| gegen | wogegen |

### da- compounds for things, not people

**Critical rule:** da- compounds refer to **things**. For **people**, use preposition + pronoun.

| Reference | Correct form |
|---|---|
| a thing | Ich warte **darauf**. |
| a person | Ich warte **auf ihn**. |

`Das ist das Buch. Ich warte **darauf**.` (thing)
`Das ist mein Freund. Ich warte **auf ihn**.` (person)

### Full example set

| German | English |
|---|---|
| Ich freue mich darauf. | I am looking forward to it. |
| Ich denke daran. | I am thinking about it. |
| Ich interessiere mich dafür. | I am interested in it. |
| Ich ärgere mich darüber. | I am annoyed about it. |
| Ich träume davon. | I dream of it. |
| Ich fange damit an. | I am starting with it. |
| Ich helfe dabei. | I help with it. |
| Ich bitte darum. | I ask for it. |

### The question form with wo-

| Question | English |
|---|---|
| Worauf wartest du? | What are you waiting for? |
| Woran denkst du? | What are you thinking about? |
| Wofür interessierst du dich? | What are you interested in? |
| Worüber ärgerst du dich? | What are you annoyed about? |
| Wovon träumst du? | What do you dream of? |
| Womit fängst du an? | What are you starting with? |
| Worum bittest du? | What are you asking for? |

### Questioning people: preposition + pronoun

For people, use the preposition + a person pronoun.

| Question | English |
|---|---|
| Auf wen wartest du? | Who are you waiting for? |
| An wen denkst du? | Who are you thinking about? |
| Für wen interessierst du dich? | Who are you interested in? |
| Mit wem sprichst du? | Who are you speaking with? |

**Pattern:** `wo(r)-` for things, `preposition + wen/wem` for people.

### Referring back with da- compounds

The da- compound often refers to a whole preceding clause.

`Er kommt zu spät. Ich ärgere mich **darüber**.` (He is late. I am annoyed about it/that.)
`Sie hat gewonnen. Ich freue mich **darüber**.` (She won. I am happy about it.)

### Common da- compounds and their meanings

| Compound | Meaning |
|---|---|
| darauf | on it, for it, to it |
| daran | on it, of it, about it |
| darüber | about it, over it |
| darum / darum | about it, for it, therefore |
| davon | of it, from it |
| damit | with it (also: so that) |
| dabei | with it, in the process |
| dafür | for it |
| dagegen | against it |
| dadurch | through it, as a result |
| darin | in it |
| dazu | to it, for it |
| danach | after it |
| davor | before it, of it |

**Careful:** `damit` has two meanings: (1) "with it" (da- compound), (2) "so that" (final conjunction). Context decides.
`Ich schreibe **damit**.` (I write with it — compound)
`Ich lerne, **damit** ich bestehe.` (I learn so that I pass — conjunction)

### Using da- compounds in answers

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Worauf wartest du? | Ich warte **darauf**. |
| Woran denkst du? | Ich denke **daran**. |
| Wofür interessierst du dich? | Ich interessiere mich **dafür**. |
| Worüber sprichst du? | Ich spreche **darüber**. |

### Stressed vs unstressed

In speech, the da- compound is stressed on the **da-** when referring back.

`Ich warte **DAR**auf.` (stressed — pointing to something)

When it introduces a clause (`darauf, dass...`), the stress shifts.

`Ich freue mich **darauf**, dass du kommst.` (I look forward to the fact that you are coming.)

### da- compound + dass clause

A da- compound can introduce a dass clause.

`Ich freue mich **darauf**, dass du kommst.` (I look forward to you coming.)
`Ich denke **daran**, dass ich lernen muss.` (I think about the fact that I must study.)

## Pronunciation note

The da- compound is stressed on **da-** when it refers back: `DArauf`, `DAran`, `DAmit`. But `daMIT` as a conjunction is stressed on the second syllable. Listen for the difference.

## Common mistakes

- Wrong: `Ich warte für es`. Right: `Ich warte darauf`.
- Wrong: using a da- compound for a person: `Ich warte darauf ihn`. Right: `Ich warte auf ihn`.
- Wrong: forgetting the r with vowel-initial prepositions: `daauf`. Right: `darauf`.
- Wrong: confusing `damit` (with it) and `damit` (so that).
- Wrong: `woauf` instead of `worauf`.
