# Lektion 10: Temporale Nebensätze: als, wenn, nachdem, bevor

**Level:** B1 | **Target time:** 30 minutes, repeat over 4 sessions | **Status:** not started

---

## Goal

Connect events in time using als, wenn, nachdem, and bevor, with the correct tense and word order.

## Why this matters

Temporal clauses are how you tell stories and sequence events. The `als` vs `wenn` distinction is a classic exam topic and a common learner error. Getting these right makes your German sound organised and clear.

---

## Material

### The four main temporal conjunctions

| Conjunction | Meaning | Use |
|---|---|---|
| **als** | when | a single event in the past |
| **wenn** | when / if | repeated events, present, or future |
| **nachdem** | after | sequence: earlier then later |
| **bevor** | before | sequence: later then earlier |

### als vs wenn: the crucial distinction

This is the most tested point in the lesson.

| Use **als** for... | Use **wenn** for... |
|---|---|
| A **single** event in the **past** | **Repeated** events |
| | Present or future |
| | "Whenever" |

**als = one time in the past**
`**Als** ich geboren wurde, schneite es.` (When I was born, it was snowing. — one event)

**wenn = every time / present / future**
`**Wenn** ich morgens aufstehe, trinke ich Kaffee.` (When I get up in the morning, I drink coffee. — repeated)
`**Wenn** ich morgen Zeit habe, rufe ich dich an.` (When I have time tomorrow, I will call you. — future)

**The mistake to avoid:** using `wenn` for a single past event.

`**Als** ich nach Berlin kam, war es kalt.` ✅ (single past event)
`**Wenn** ich nach Berlin kam, war es kalt.` ❌ (unless it happened repeatedly)

### als with the past tense

`als` is followed by the Präteritum (or Plusquamperfekt).

`**Als** ich klein **war**, wohnte ich in Jakarta.` (When I was small, I lived in Jakarta.)
`**Als** er ankam, warteten wir schon.` (When he arrived, we were already waiting.)

### wenn with present, future, and repeated past

`**Wenn** es **regnet**, bleibe ich zu Hause.` (When it rains, I stay home. — present, repeated)
`**Wenn** ich Zeit **habe**, komme ich.` (When I have time, I will come. — future)
`**Immer wenn** ich ihn **sah**, lächelte er.` (Whenever I saw him, he smiled. — repeated past)

**Note:** `immer wenn` or `jedes Mal wenn` makes the repetition explicit.

### nachdem: sequence with different tenses

`nachdem` marks the **earlier** event. The `nachdem` clause takes the Plusquamperfekt (for past) or the Perfekt (for present/future).

| nachdem clause | main clause |
|---|---|
| Plusquamperfekt | Präteritum / Perfekt |
| Perfekt | Präsens / Futur |

`**Nachdem** ich gegessen **hatte**, ging ich spazieren.` (After I had eaten, I went for a walk.)
`**Nachdem** ich gegessen **habe**, gehe ich spazieren.` (After I eat, I go for a walk. — present)

**The rule:** the `nachdem` clause is always one step further back in time than the main clause.

### bevor: the reverse sequence

`bevor` marks the **later** event, so the main clause is the earlier one.

`**Bevor** ich zur Arbeit **gehe**, frühstücke ich.` (Before I go to work, I have breakfast.)

Order: breakfast (first) → work (second). The `bevor` clause is the later event.

`**Bevor** er **kam**, hatte ich schon gegessen.` (Before he came, I had already eaten.)

### Word order: verb to the end

All four are subordinating conjunctions, so the verb goes to the **end** of the subordinate clause.

`**Als** ich klein **war**, ...` ✅ (`war` at the end)
`**Nachdem** ich gegessen **hatte**, ...` ✅ (`hatte` at the end)
`**Bevor** ich **gehe**, ...` ✅

**Main clause order after the subordinate clause:** if the subordinate clause comes first, the main clause starts with its **verb** (position 1 is taken by the whole subordinate clause).

`**Als** ich klein war, **wohnte** ich in Jakarta.` (verb `wohnte` right after the comma)

### Subordinate clause after the main clause

`Ich wohnte in Jakarta, **als** ich klein war.` (main clause first, then the subordinate)

Both orders are correct. Putting the subordinate clause first emphasises it.

### Tense overview

| Conjunction | Typical tenses |
|---|---|
| als | Präteritum, Plusquamperfekt |
| wenn | Präsens, Futur, Präteritum (repeated) |
| nachdem | Plusquamperfekt (past), Perfekt (present) |
| bevor | any; often Präsens or Präteritum |

### Common patterns and phrases

| German | English |
|---|---|
| als ich ein Kind war | when I was a child |
| als ich jung war | when I was young |
| jedes Mal, wenn | every time that |
| immer wenn | whenever |
| nachdem ich angekommen war | after I had arrived |
| bevor ich gehe | before I go |
| kurz bevor | shortly before |
| gleich nachdem | right after |

### The als / wenn decision tree

1. Is it a single event in the past? → **als**
2. Is it repeated, present, or future? → **wenn**
3. Is it "if" (conditional)? → **wenn** (but that is a conditional clause, a different function)

### Worked example: a short story

`**Als** ich gestern aufwachte, war es schon hell. **Nachdem** ich gefrühstückt hatte, ging ich zur Arbeit. **Bevor** ich das Haus verließ, rief ich meine Mutter an. **Wenn** ich früh ankomme, trinke ich einen Kaffee.`

(When I woke up yesterday, it was already light. After I had had breakfast, I went to work. Before I left the house, I called my mother. When I arrive early, I drink a coffee.)

Notice: `als` (single past), `nachdem` (Plusquamperfekt), `bevor` (before), `wenn` (repeated/present).

## Pronunciation note

`als` and `wenn` are both short and unstressed in the clause. The stress falls on the content word: `Als ich KLEIN war`. Practise the comma pause: a small break marks the clause boundary.

## Common mistakes

- Wrong: `Wenn ich nach Berlin kam, ...` for a single past event. Right: `Als`.
- Wrong: `Als es regnet, ...` for a repeated event. Right: `Wenn`.
- Wrong: `Nachdem ich gegessen habe, ging ich...` (past). Right: `Nachdem ich gegessen hatte, ...`.
- Wrong: verb in position 2 after the subordinate clause: `..., ich wohnte`. Right: `..., wohnte ich`.
- Wrong: forgetting that `nachdem` needs a tense shift.
