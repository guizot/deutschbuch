# Lektion 14: Der Genitiv in seiner vollen Verwendung

**Level:** B1 | **Target time:** 30 minutes, repeat over 3 sessions | **Status:** not started

---

## Goal

Use the genitive confidently for possession, after genitive prepositions, and in formal writing.

## Why this matters

At A2 you learned to recognise the genitive. At B1 you use it. It is the mark of formal, educated German, and it appears in news, contracts, literature, and formal letters. The genitive prepositions (wegen, trotz, während, statt) are everyday and tested.

---

## Material

### The genitive: a reminder

The genitive expresses possession and answers "whose?" (Wessen?).

`das Auto **des Mannes**` (the man's car)
`die Farbe **der Wand**` (the colour of the wall)

### Genitive articles

| | Masculine | Feminine | Neuter | Plural |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Definite | des | der | des | der |
| Indefinite | eines | einer | eines | - |
| Possessive | meines | meiner | meines | meiner |
| Negative | keines | keiner | keines | keiner |

### The masculine/neuter -s / -es ending

Masculine and neuter nouns add **-s** or **-es**.

| Nominative | Genitive |
|---|---|
| der Mann | des Mann**es** |
| das Kind | des Kind**es** |
| der Lehrer | des Lehrer**s** |
| das Auto | des Auto**s** |
| der Freund | des Freund**es** |
| das Haus | des Haus**es** |

**-es** for one-syllable nouns and those ending in -s, -ss, -ß, -z, -tz, -sch. **-s** for longer nouns.

### Weak nouns (n-Deklination) in the genitive

Some masculine nouns add **-en** or **-n** in the genitive (and accusative, and dative). These are the "weak" nouns.

| Nominative | Genitive | Type |
|---|---|---|
| der Student | des Student**en** | weak |
| der Junge | des Junge**n** | weak |
| der Mensch | des Mensch**en** | weak |
| der Name | des Name**ns** | weak (+ns) |
| der Herr | des Herr**n** | weak |
| der Nachbar | des Nachbar**n** | weak |
| der Kollege | des Kollege**n** | weak |
| der Franzose | des Franzose**n** | weak |

You will study n-Deklination in detail in Lektion 16. For now, note that these nouns take -en/-n in the genitive.

### Genitive prepositions

These prepositions take the genitive.

| Preposition | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| wegen | because of | wegen **des** Wetters |
| trotz | despite | trotz **der** Kälte |
| während | during | während **des** Tages |
| statt / anstatt | instead of | statt **des** Autos |
| außerhalb | outside of | außerhalb **der** Stadt |
| innerhalb | within | innerhalb **einer** Woche |
| aufgrund | due to | aufgrund **des** Regens |
| dank | thanks to | dank **deiner** Hilfe |
| laut | according to | laut **des** Berichts |
| bezüglich | regarding | bezüglich **der** Frage |
| hinsichtlich | regarding | hinsichtlich **der** Kosten |
| anlässlich | on the occasion of | anlässlich **des** Jubiläums |
| infolge | as a result of | infolge **des** Unfalls |
| trotz | despite | trotz **des** Regens |

**Note:** `dank` is often used with the dative in casual speech (`dank dir`), but the genitive is correct in formal writing (`dank deiner Hilfe`).

### Genitive with names

Add **-s** to names (no article).

`**Rinaldis** Auto` (Rinaldi's car)
`**Annas** Buch` (Anna's book)

Names ending in -s take an apostrophe: `**Hans'** Auto`.

### Genitive vs von + dative

| Genitive (formal) | von + Dativ (spoken) |
|---|---|
| das Auto des Mannes | das Auto von dem Mann |
| die Farbe der Wand | die Farbe von der Wand |

Both are correct. The genitive is formal and preferred in writing.

### Genitive in formal writing

The genitive is a hallmark of formal, written German.

`Die Hauptstadt **Deutschlands** ist Berlin.` (The capital of Germany is Berlin.)
`Die Ursache **des Problems** war ein Fehler.` (The cause of the problem was a mistake.)
`Der Präsident **der Universität** sprach.` (The president of the university spoke.)
`Trotz **des Regens** fand das Fest statt.` (Despite the rain, the festival took place.)

### Genitive relative pronouns

`dessen` (masculine/neuter) and `deren` (feminine/plural).

`Der Mann, **dessen** Auto gestohlen wurde, ...` (The man whose car was stolen...)
`Die Frau, **deren** Buch ich lese, ...` (The woman whose book I am reading...)
`Die Leute, **deren** Haus brennt, ...` (The people whose house is burning...)

### Common fixed genitive expressions

| German | English |
|---|---|
| meiner Meinung nach | in my opinion |
| eines Tages | one day |
| letzten Endes | in the end |
| dieser Tage | these days |
| aller Art | of all kinds |
| trotz aller Schwierigkeiten | despite all difficulties |
| aufgrund der Tatsache, dass | due to the fact that |

### Genitive in academic and formal registers

| Function | Example |
|---|---|
| Defining | die Bedeutung des Wortes |
| Naming | das Ende des Films |
| Measuring | die Dauer des Kurses |
| Attributing | die Werke Goethes |

### The genitive with adjectives

Some adjectives govern the genitive.

| Adjective | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| bewusst | aware of | sich einer Sache bewusst sein |
| sicher | sure of | einer Sache sicher sein |
| würdig | worthy of | einer Sache würdig sein |
| verdächtig | suspected of | einer Sache verdächtig sein |
| mächtig | in command of | einer Sprache mächtig sein |

`Ich bin **meiner Sache** sicher.` (I am sure of my cause.)
`Er ist **des Verbrechens** verdächtig.` (He is suspected of the crime.)

### Practical B1 advice

1. **Use the genitive** in formal writing: letters, reports, essays.
2. **Use von + dative** in casual speech if you are unsure.
3. **Learn the genitive prepositions** as fixed units: wegen + Gen, trotz + Gen.
4. **Watch weak nouns**: der Student → des Studenten.

## Pronunciation note

`wegen` is "VAY-gen", `trotz` is "trots", `während` is "VAY-rent", `aufgrund` is "OWF-grunt". The genitive article `des` is short and unstressed.

## Common mistakes

- Wrong: `wegen das Wetter`. Right: `wegen des Wetters`.
- Wrong: `Anna's Buch`. Right: `Annas Buch`.
- Wrong: forgetting the -s/-es: `des Mann`. Right: `des Mannes`.
- Wrong: treating weak nouns like strong ones: `des Student`. Right: `des Studenten`.
- Wrong: using the dative after genitive prepositions in formal writing.
