# Wortschatz B1: Vocabulary

Target: **~2,400 words** (Goethe-Zertifikat B1 Wortliste). This is the **last official Goethe list**; from B2 up, vocabulary is open and topic-driven.

This file lists the vocabulary that typically appears at B1, grouped by theme. Combined with A1 (~650) and A2 (~1,300), you reach roughly the B1 target.

## How to use this file

- Learn nouns **with article and plural**.
- Learn verbs **with their preposition and case** (`warten auf + Akk`).
- Learn adjectives **with a typical noun** they describe.
- Tick the box when the word is in your Anki deck.
