# Resources

Everything here is either free or has a free tier that is genuinely usable. Paid items are marked.

## Official and free (start here)

| Resource | What it is | Level | Link |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Nicos Weg** (Deutsche Welle) | Full video course with exercises. The best free structured course that exists | A1-B1 | dw.com/en/learn-german/nicos-weg |
| **Goethe Wortliste A1** | Official vocabulary list, ~650 words | A1 | goethe.de (search "A1 Wortliste PDF") |
| **Goethe Wortliste A2** | Official vocabulary list, ~1,300 words | A2 | goethe.de |
| **Goethe Wortliste B1** | Official vocabulary list, ~2,400 words. Last official list | B1 | goethe.de |
| **Goethe mock exams** | Free practice exams per level | A1-C2 | goethe.de/en/spr/prf.html |
| **telc mock exams** | Free practice exams, especially B1/B2 | A1-C1 | telc.net |
| **DW Deutsch lernen** | Podcasts, slowly spoken news | A2-C1 | dw.com/de/deutsch-lernen |
| **Lingolia** | Grammar reference, clearly explained | A1-C1 | deutsch.lingolia.com |
| **LEO dictionary** | Dictionary with declension and conjugation tables | all | dict.leo.org |
| **dict.cc** | Dictionary, strong for phrases | all | dict.cc |

| Book | Notes |
|---|---|
| **Schritte international neu** | A1-B1. Very common in courses. Good structure |
| **Menschen** | A1-B1. Hueber. Clean layout, strong exercises |
| **Netzwerk** | A1-B1. Klett. Communicative focus |

## Speaking practice

Speaking is the skill self-study neglects and exams test hardest. Start at A2.

| Platform | Notes |
|---|---|
| **iTalki** | Paid tutors, from cheap community tutors to professionals. Book 30 min |
| **Preply** | Similar to iTalki |
| **Tandem** | Free language exchange with native speakers |
| **HelloTalk** | Free, chat-based exchange |
| **Goethe-Institut courses** | Paid, structured, gives you the certificate track |
| **VHS (Volkshochschule)** | Cheap local courses in Germany, if you are already there |

## Anki decks

Search AnkiWeb for:
- `Goethe A1 Wortliste` (several exist, check ratings)
- `German 4000 words` (frequency-ordered, good for B1 onward)
- `German grammar drills`

Better than downloading: build your own deck from the words you actually meet. Words you add yourself stick harder.

## Suggested free-only stack

If you want zero cost, this is a complete path:

1. **Nicos Weg** for the course structure (A1-B1)
2. **Goethe Wortlisten PDFs** for vocabulary targets
3. **Anki** for spaced repetition
4. **Lingolia** for grammar reference
5. **Easy German** for listening
6. **Tandem** or **HelloTalk** for speaking
7. **Goethe mock exams** for checkpoints
