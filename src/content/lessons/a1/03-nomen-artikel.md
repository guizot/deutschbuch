# Lektion 03: Nomen, Genus und Artikel (Nouns, Gender and Article)

**Level:** A1 | **Target time:** 30 minutes, repeat over 4 sessions | **Status:** not started

---

## Goal

Know that every German noun has a gender, learn to store nouns with their article, and learn the patterns that let you guess gender correctly most of the time.

## Why this matters

**This is the most important lesson in A1.** Everything later depends on it: Akkusativ, Dativ, adjective endings, relative pronouns. All of it needs the gender.

English speakers often learn `Tisch = table` and move on. That is the mistake that causes a wall at B1. Learn `der Tisch` from the start. It costs the same effort now and saves enormous pain later.

---

## Material

### Every noun has a gender

German nouns are **masculine (der)**, **feminine (die)**, or **neuter (das)**. This is grammatical gender, not biological, and it is largely arbitrary. `das Mädchen` (the girl) is neuter. `der Löffel` (the spoon) is masculine. There is no logic to fix by thinking harder.

| Article | Gender | Example |
|---|---|---|
| der | masculine | der Tisch (table), der Mann (man), der Tag (day) |
| die | feminine | die Tür (door), die Frau (woman), die Zeit (time) |
| das | neuter | das Kind (child), das Buch (book), das Haus (house) |

### The plural article is always die

| Singular | Plural |
|---|---|
| der Tisch | die Tische |
| die Tür | die Türen |
| das Kind | die Kinder |

Whatever the singular gender, the plural article is always **die**. This is the one piece of good news.

### How to write vocabulary from now on

Never write a noun alone. Always:

```
der Tisch, -e        (table)
die Tür, -en         (door)
das Kind, -er        (child)
das Mädchen, -        (girl, no plural change)
```

The `-e` / `-en` / `-er` shows the plural ending. `-` means the plural is the same as the singular.

### Gender patterns (useful guesses)

Gender is not fully predictable, but these patterns are reliable enough to guess correctly most of the time.

**Masculine (der):**

| Pattern | Examples | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Days, months, seasons | der Montag, der Januar, der Sommer | Always masculine |
| Weather | der Regen, der Schnee, der Wind | |
| -er (people and agents, from verbs) | der Lehrer, der Arbeiter, der Computer | Careful: not all -er words |
| -ling | der Frühling, der Lehrling | |
| -or | der Motor, der Doktor | |
| -ismus | der Tourismus | |
| Alcoholic drinks | der Wein, der Bier (but das Bier in some regions) | der Wein, der Schnaps |

**Feminine (die):**

| Pattern | Examples |
|---|---|
| -ung | die Zeitung, die Wohnung, die Übung |
| -heit | die Freiheit, die Gesundheit |
| -keit | die Möglichkeit, die Schwierigkeit |
| -schaft | die Freundschaft, die Mannschaft |
| -tion | die Information, die Nation |
| -tät | die Universität, die Aktivität |
| -ie | die Familie, die Industrie |
| -in (female people) | die Lehrerin, die Freundin |
| Most nouns ending in -e | die Blume, die Schule, die Straße |

**Neuter (das):**

| Pattern | Examples |
|---|---|
| Diminutives -chen, -lein | das Mädchen, das Brötchen, das Fräulein |
| -ment | das Dokument, das Instrument |
| -um | das Museum, das Zentrum |
| -nis | das Ergebnis, das Zeugnis |
| Infinitives used as nouns | das Essen, das Lesen, das Schwimmen |
| Young beings | das Kind, das Baby |

### Noun capitalisation

**All German nouns are capitalised.** Always. `der Tisch`, `die Stadt`, `Ich trinke Kaffee`. This is not optional and it makes German text easy to scan.

### Compound nouns

German glues words together. The gender of the compound comes from the **last** word.

| Compound | Parts | Gender |
|---|---|---|
| das Schlafzimmer | Schlaf + Zimmer | das (Zimmer) |
| der Haustürschlüssel | Haus + Tür + Schlüssel | der (Schlüssel) |
| die Krankenhausverwaltung | Krankenhaus + Verwaltung | die (Verwaltung) |

### Negative article: kein

| | Masculine | Feminine | Neuter | Plural |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Nominative | kein Tisch | keine Tür | kein Kind | keine Kinder |
| Accusative | keinen Tisch | keine Tür | kein Kind | keine Kinder |

## Exercises

**1. Which article?** der, die, or das?

a) ______ Buch
b) ______ Wohnung
c) ______ Mädchen
d) ______ Lehrer
e) ______ Zeitung
f) ______ Fenster
g) ______ Freundschaft
h) ______ Museum
i) ______ Küche
j) ______ Auto

**2. Guess by pattern.** Use the patterns above. Which article?

a) ______ Möglichkeit
b) ______ Dokument
c) ______ Übung
d) ______ Frühling
e) ______ Universität
f) ______ Brötchen
g) ______ Regen
h) ______ Familie

**3. Plural form.** Give the plural with its article.

a) der Tisch → ______
b) die Tür → ______
c) das Kind → ______
d) das Haus → ______
e) die Stadt → ______
f) der Zug → ______

**4. Compound gender.** What is the article of the compound?

a) Schlafzimmer (Schlaf + Zimmer)
b) Küchentür (Küche + Tür)
c) Haustürschlüssel (Haus + Tür + Schlüssel)
d) Kinderbuch (Kinder + Buch)

**5. kein or keine?** Fill in.

a) Ich habe ______ Zeit. (die Zeit)
b) Das ist ______ Tisch. (der Tisch)
c) Ich habe ______ Kinder. (die Kinder)
d) Das ist ______ Problem. (das Problem)

**6. Correct the sentence.** Add the missing articles.

a) Tisch ist groß.
b) Ich habe Wohnung in Stadt.
c) Kind spielt mit Buch.

g) die Freundschaft (-schaft)
h) das Museum (-um)

d) der Frühling (-ling)

a) das Schlafzimmer (from Zimmer)
b) die Küchentür (from Tür)

## Pronunciation note

`die` is "dee", `der` is "dair" (with the soft -er), `das` is "dahs". When you say them quickly in a sentence they almost blur together, so listen for the vowel.

## Common mistakes

- Wrong: learning `Tisch = table`. Right: learn `der Tisch, die Tische`.
- Wrong: thinking `das Mädchen` is a mistake because it means "girl". Right: it is correct. -chen is always neuter.
- Wrong: writing `der tisch` in lowercase. Right: all nouns are capitalised: `der Tisch`.
- Wrong: assuming the plural keeps the gender. Right: the plural article is always `die`.
