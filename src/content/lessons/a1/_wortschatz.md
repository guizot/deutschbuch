# Wortschatz A1: Vocabulary by Topic

Organised by the **10 official Goethe topic areas** of the A1 Wortliste. The official list contains about **650 words**. You need roughly **half of them actively** (able to produce), the rest only receptively (able to understand).

## How to use this file

- Learn nouns **with their article and plural**. Always.
- Tick the box when the word is in your Anki deck and you can recall it.
- Do not try to learn all 650 at once. Add them as you meet them in lessons, Nicos Weg, and Easy German.
